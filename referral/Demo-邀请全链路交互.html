设备端为什么只有二维码Why the device only shows QR

圆屏没有浏览器、分享面板、键盘。它能做的只有把一个二维码放大到中间，让好友当场扫（C2）或让自己扫回手机（C4）。设备端全部就是"入口 → 二维码 → 反馈"三步。The round screen has no browser, share sheet or keyboard. All it can do is put one QR in the middle for a friend to scan (C2) or for you to scan back to your phone (C4). The whole device side is "entry → QR → feedback".

圆屏排版三条规则Three rules for the round screen

  • 内容落在内接正方形 330×330 里（466/√2），四角不放东西。Content stays inside the inscribed 330×330 square (466/√2); nothing in the corners.
  • 一屏只放一件事：标题 + 一个主体 + 一个动作。One thing per screen: a title, one subject, one action.
  • 字号 ≥ 15px（466 尺度），二维码 ≥ 220px。Type ≥ 15 px at 466 scale; QR ≥ 220 px.

好友侧两个分支Two branches on the friend side

想买的从 B2 直接去 B3；犹豫的去 B5 注册把钱存起来，我们拿到一个能推送的线索。B5 是 lead capture，不是奖励，对邀请人只计数不发奖。Ready buyers go B2 → B3; hesitant ones go to B5, bank the credit, and give us a lead we can nudge. B5 is lead capture, not a reward — it only counts for the inviter.
